## 1 · איך נבנה הבלוק — הדלתא, סעיף-סעיף

הבלוק שבסעיף 4 הוא הבלוק הקבוע (`media-review/PROMPT-PASTE.md`)
עם **דלתת שלב 1 בלבד**, כלשון `MADREG §5`:

| פעולה | סעיפים |
|---|---|
| **הוחלף** (נוסח שלב 1, מילה במילה מ-MADREG §5) | `WHAT THIS IS` · `PALETTE` · `TEXTURE` |
| **נמחק** (הסעיף כולו) | `ACCENT DISCIPLINE` |
| **נוסף** (מילה במילה מ-MADREG §5) | `ADDITIONAL NEGATIVE FOR THIS STAGE` |
| **לא שוּנה — בית-בית** | כל השאר: `LIGHT` · `CAMERA` · `ROOFS` · `SKY` · `DEPTH BY INTERIOR SHADOW ONLY` · `NOTHING CASTS A SHADOW…` · `FOREGROUND` · `FIGURES` · `SCALE` · `ASPECT` · `NEGATIVE` · `NO WRITING…` · `OUTPUT` |

**שתי הצבות הרצה בלבד** (פרמטרים מוגדרים, `RUN-SHEET-OPS.md
§"שלוש ההצבות"`): ‏`FOUR (4)` ← `TWO (2)` בהוראת הפלט, ו-
`four distinct image files … four pictures` ← `two … two`.

**והכרעה פרשנית אחת, מסומנת לביקורת העיצוב:** שורת הפתיחה של
הבלוק — *"identical for every plate and must never vary between
plates"* — אינה בין ששת הסעיפים הזזים ולכן נשארה בית-בית. בתוך
הרצה אחת היא אמת (שני הלוחות באותו שלב); על פני הסולם כולו היא
מתייחסת מעתה ללוחות של אותו שלב. אם צוות העיצוב מעדיף נוסח מפורש
(למשל "identical for every plate of this stage") — זו נגיעה בסעיף
קפוא ולכן טעונה אישור אדמין, ואנו מסמנים אותה כשאלה, לא מכריעים.

## 2 · תיקון תוכן אחד, מסומן ומנומק

**לוח 6, `PERIOD MATERIAL`: ‏`hand-made unglazed pottery` ←
`wheel-made unglazed pottery`.** זה אינו סגנון אלא תיקון עובדתי
שכבר יושב ברשומת הלוח עצמו: ה-rationale המאומת של `ATLAS-06`
(סעיף "החרס") קובע — רפרטואר הברונזה התיכונה II הוא כלי אובניים,
והאובניים המהיר מוצג בכנען ב-MB II, *"ולכן 'בעבודת יד' מייחס
לתקופה טכנולוגיה שאינה שלה"* (מקורות מאומתים: Amiran 1969; Uziel,
Rosetta 10 [2011]). הפרומפט המקורי קדם לבדיקה הזאת; ההרצה מחדש
היא ההזדמנות לסגור את הפסק גם בפרומפט (חוק סגירת הפסק, 22.8).
שום מילה אחרת בשני בלוקי הלוחות לא שונתה.

## 4 · מה מדביקים — הודעה אחת, מ-`ATLAS PLATE` עד הסוף

```
ATLAS PLATE — FIXED STYLE. This style block is identical for every
plate and must never vary between plates.

WHAT THIS IS: a schematic reconstruction diagram, engraved rather than
painted. It shows a PATTERN of life — how people in this land lived at
this stage — and never a particular day, a particular place, or a
particular person.

PALETTE: two inks only — parchment cream and one deep brown. NO accent
colour anywhere in the image, not on vegetation and not on anything
else.

LIGHT: flat, diffuse daylight. No sunset, no sunrise, no golden hour,
no rim light, no dramatic shadow, no visible light source.

CAMERA: eye level, very slightly elevated — the height of a person
standing on the ground. NEVER a bird's-eye view, never looking down into
a building, never an aerial or map-like view. If the whole structure
does not fit at eye level, show only the part that fits.

ROOFS: every building that had a roof is drawn WITH its roof intact.
Do NOT draw a cut-away, a cross-section, or a roofless plan view of any
building, EXCEPT where a plate explicitly asks for one.

SKY: wherever sky is visible it is bare parchment cream, the same tone
as the paper, with no gradient and no cloud. NEVER blue, never tinted,
never atmospheric.

DEPTH BY INTERIOR SHADOW ONLY: the plate is drawn, not pale — but all
of its darkness lives INSIDE forms, never beside them. Go to a deep
near-black brown in these places and these only: the black of an open
doorway or window; the recess under a roof eave or awning; the joints
between stones; the underside of a ledge, step or lintel; the inside of
a niche; the mouth of a jar; the hollow of a rock-cut opening. Model
volume with hatching and cross-hatching, the way an engraved plate
does. Every outward-facing surface is evenly lit across its whole
width.

NOTHING CASTS A SHADOW ONTO ANYTHING ELSE. Specifically, and none of
these may appear: no shadow of an object on the ground or paving beside
it; no shadow of an object on a wall behind it; no diagonal band of
shade across a floor, a wall or a courtyard; no shadow of a roof edge,
awning, fence or parapet; no shadow under or beside a jar, chest,
bench or vessel; no dark side and light side on the same wall. There is
no sun direction anywhere in this image.

FOREGROUND: stage the near edge of the scene deliberately — rocks,
scrub, a broken vessel, the lip of a terrace — drawn with finer detail
and deeper tone than the middle distance, so the eye enters the plate
at the bottom and travels up into it.

FIGURES: only where needed to give scale. Then small, seen from
behind or in profile, never facing the viewer, never a portrait.

SCALE: any human figure stands, kneels or crawls ON THE GROUND at
correct human scale — a head shorter than a doorway, taller than a
storage jar. Never standing on a bench, a wall or furniture. Never
smaller than the vessels beside it.

TEXTURE: visible engraving throughout. All modelling is line: hatching
and cross-hatching only. No continuous wash, no blended tone, no
painted surface anywhere.

ASPECT: 3:2, horizontal.

NEGATIVE — none of these may appear: photorealism, photograph,
cinematic lighting, golden hour, sunset, sunrise, lens flare, bokeh,
shallow depth of field, wide-angle distortion, dust motes, god rays,
faces toward the viewer, portraits, modern objects, epic scale,
dramatic sky, invented flags, invented emblems, heraldry, epic
atmosphere, saturated colour, HDR, tilt-shift, vignette, aerial view,
bird's-eye view, isometric view, floor plan.

ADDITIONAL NEGATIVE FOR THIS STAGE: colour of any kind beyond the two
inks; painted or blended tone; individual facial features; any
identifiable person; any attempt to portray a named figure of the
narrative.

NO WRITING IN ANY IMAGE, AND NO PLATE NUMBER ANYWHERE. The image
contains no letters, no numerals, no words, no labels, no captions,
no titles, no signature, no watermark, no scale bar and no compass
rose. IN PARTICULAR: THIS IMAGE IS NOT LABELLED WITH ITS OWN NUMBER.
Do not write the word "Plate", the word "Figure", or any number, in
any corner, in any margin, along any edge, inside or outside the
drawn frame. The paper margin outside the ruled frame is blank
parchment and nothing whatever appears in it. Where an ancient
inscription is part of a depicted object, render it as illegible
incised marks only - never as readable letters.

OUTPUT — READ THIS CAREFULLY BEFORE GENERATING ANYTHING.

Produce TWO (2) SEPARATE, INDEPENDENT IMAGES.

This means two distinct image files. It does NOT mean one image
containing two pictures. Do NOT produce a grid, a collage, a contact
sheet, a mood board, a storyboard, a diptych, a triptych, a
side-by-side comparison, a single canvas divided into panels, a sheet
of thumbnails, or any single image containing more than one scene.

Each image contains exactly ONE scene and fills its own complete 3:2
frame, edge to edge.

Generate them ONE AT A TIME, in the order given below. After each
image, state in your written reply which plate it is — in the reply
text only, never inside the image.

Do not write "this concludes", "all images have been generated", "no
plates were skipped", or any similar statement.

End your final reply with two plain lists: the plate numbers you
actually produced as images, and separately, any you did not produce.

PLATE 6.

SUBJECT: a herding camp on open hill country. Two or three low black
goat-hair tents, guyed to the ground and open along one side, stand
beside a stone-lined well with a worn drinking trough next to it. A
flock of sheep and goats is gathered at the trough. Storage jars and
folded bedding lie under the tent awning.

PERIOD MATERIAL: wheel-made unglazed pottery, bronze tools, goat-hair
cloth, rope, waterskins, a stone well-head worn smooth by rope.

SETTING: open rolling hill country, dry grass, scattered terebinth
trees, low hills to the horizon. No buildings anywhere.

ADDITIONAL NEGATIVE FOR THIS PLATE: camels, a caravan, a walled city,
a town, permanent buildings, a campfire, a night scene, a family
portrait, anyone facing the viewer.

DISTINGUISHING FEATURES (all must be present): black low tents open
along one side; a stone-lined well with a worn trough; a flock at the
trough; NO permanent building anywhere in the frame.

PLATE 7.

SUBJECT: a dense courtyard between rows of small mud-brick workers'
dwellings. Bricks are drying in a rectangular wooden mould in the
foreground, straw visible in the mix; a stack of finished bricks
stands beside it. Flat roofs, narrow lanes, plain water jars against
the walls.

PERIOD MATERIAL: mud brick with chopped straw, wooden brick moulds,
wheel-made pottery, developed bronze tools, reed matting.

SETTING: flat delta ground, a canal edge with reeds in the distance,
date palms. No hills.

ADDITIONAL NEGATIVE FOR THIS PLATE: pyramids, temples, monumental
statues, hieroglyphs, obelisks, pharaonic imagery, whips, overseers,
chains, a scene of violence, gold.

DISTINGUISHING FEATURES (all must be present): a wooden brick mould
with wet brick in it, in the foreground; chopped straw clearly visible
in the brick; dense rows of small flat-roofed dwellings; flat delta
ground with no hills.
```

## 7 · אחרי ההרצה — מה שמחכה בצד שלנו

ביקורת קבלה (דרגה א׳/ב׳ לפי `PLATE-RATIONALE §8`) + מדידות +
סיידקאר רוויה (‏§8ד) + ‏`rationale` מעודכן לשפת שלב 1, ובו שורת
ה-`uncertain` המחייבת של השלב: *"אינו מקום מסוים ואינו אדם מסוים"*
(‏§6). ההחלפה באתר — רק אחרי פסק המדיה על המועמדים.
