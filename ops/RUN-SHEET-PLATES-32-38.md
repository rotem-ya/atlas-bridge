   — האיסור על פנים ועל דמות בולטת מוחלט. בבמה מוחשכת דמות אחת

```
ATLAS PLATE — FIXED STYLE. This style block is identical for every
plate and must never vary between plates.

WHAT THIS IS: a restrained documentary reconstruction plate, of the
kind printed in a scholarly historical atlas. It is a drawn plate,
not a photograph and not concept art.

PALETTE: muted earth tones only — parchment cream, deep brown ink,
and ONE desaturated accent. No colour outside that range.

ACCENT DISCIPLINE: the single accent is a muted olive-sage green, used
only for vegetation. Nothing else in the image carries colour. Clothing,
water, roofs and stone stay within the cream-to-brown range. No blue,
no teal, no turquoise anywhere. The figures must never be the most
saturated thing in the frame.

LIGHT: flat, diffuse daylight. No sunset, no sunrise, no golden hour,
no rim light, no dramatic shadow, no visible light source.

CAMERA: eye level, very slightly elevated — the height of a person
standing on the ground. NEVER a bird's-eye view, never looking down into
a building, never an aerial or map-like view. If the whole structure
does not fit at eye level, show only the part that fits.

ROOFS: every building that had a roof is drawn WITH its roof intact.
Do NOT draw a cut-away, a cross-section, or a roofless plan view of any
building, EXCEPT where a plate explicitly asks for one.

SKY: wherever sky is visible it is bare parchment cream, the same tone
as the paper, with no gradient and no cloud. NEVER blue, never tinted,
never atmospheric.

DEPTH BY INTERIOR SHADOW ONLY: the plate is drawn, not pale — but all
of its darkness lives INSIDE forms, never beside them. Go to a deep
near-black brown in these places and these only: the black of an open
doorway or window; the recess under a roof eave or awning; the joints
between stones; the underside of a ledge, step or lintel; the inside of
a niche; the mouth of a jar; the hollow of a rock-cut opening. Model
volume with hatching and cross-hatching, the way an engraved plate
does. Every outward-facing surface is evenly lit across its whole
width.

NOTHING CASTS A SHADOW ONTO ANYTHING ELSE. Specifically, and none of
these may appear: no shadow of an object on the ground or paving beside
it; no shadow of an object on a wall behind it; no diagonal band of
shade across a floor, a wall or a courtyard; no shadow of a roof edge,
awning, fence or parapet; no shadow under or beside a jar, chest,
bench or vessel; no dark side and light side on the same wall. There is
no sun direction anywhere in this image.

FOREGROUND: stage the near edge of the scene deliberately — rocks,
scrub, a broken vessel, the lip of a terrace — drawn with finer detail
and deeper tone than the middle distance, so the eye enters the plate
at the bottom and travels up into it.

FIGURES: only where needed to give scale. Then small, seen from
behind or in profile, never facing the viewer, never a portrait.

SCALE: any human figure stands, kneels or crawls ON THE GROUND at
correct human scale — a head shorter than a doorway, taller than a
storage jar. Never standing on a bench, a wall or furniture. Never
smaller than the vessels beside it.

TEXTURE: fine, even grain across the whole image, like the tooth of
printed paper.

ASPECT: 3:2, horizontal.

NEGATIVE — none of these may appear: photorealism, photograph,
cinematic lighting, golden hour, sunset, sunrise, lens flare, bokeh,
shallow depth of field, wide-angle distortion, dust motes, god rays,
faces toward the viewer, portraits, modern objects, epic scale,
dramatic sky, invented flags, invented emblems, heraldry, epic
atmosphere, saturated colour, HDR, tilt-shift, vignette, aerial view,
bird's-eye view, isometric view, floor plan.

NO WRITING IN ANY IMAGE. The images contain no letters, numbers,
words, labels, captions, titles, plate numbers, signatures,
watermarks, scale bars or compass roses. Do not write a plate
number, an "ATLAS-nn" code, or any variant of either, in any corner
or along any edge. Where an
ancient inscription is part of a depicted object, render it as
illegible incised marks only — never as readable letters.

OUTPUT — READ THIS CAREFULLY BEFORE GENERATING ANYTHING.

Produce FOUR (4) SEPARATE, INDEPENDENT IMAGES.

This means four distinct image files. It does NOT mean one image
containing four pictures. Do NOT produce a grid, a collage, a contact
sheet, a mood board, a storyboard, a diptych, a triptych, a
side-by-side comparison, a single canvas divided into panels, a sheet
of thumbnails, or any single image containing more than one scene.

Each image contains exactly ONE scene and fills its own complete 3:2
frame, edge to edge.

Generate them ONE AT A TIME, in the order given below. After each
image, state in your written reply which plate it is — in the reply
text only, never inside the image.

Do not write "this concludes", "all images have been generated", "no
plates were skipped", or any similar statement.

End your final reply with two plain lists: the plate numbers you
actually produced as images, and separately, any you did not produce.

PLATE 32.

THE CLAIM OF THIS PLATE: this is a place before anything happens in
it. There is no event, no person, no army, no ruin and no fortress
anywhere in the frame — only worked farmland on a hill. A viewer must
be able to look at it and feel that nothing has gone wrong yet.

SUBJECT: hill country seen at eye level from one slope across a
shallow valley to the next. The near and middle slopes are cut into
long stone-walled agricultural terraces following the contours. On
one terrace, rows of low vines on the ground. Scattered olive trees.
On the far ridge, a small village of flat-roofed stone houses, seen
at a distance and quite small in the frame — no wall around it, no
tower, no gate. Bare stony hilltops beyond.

PERIOD MATERIAL: dry-stone terrace walls of undressed field stone,
flat-roofed rubble-built houses, low bush-trained vines, olive trees,
a beaten earth path.

SETTING: Judaean hill country. Bare parchment sky. Olive-sage green
only in the vines, olives and scrub.

ADDITIONAL NEGATIVE FOR THIS PLATE: any human figure, any animal, a
city wall, a tower, a fortress, a camp, soldiers, weapons, a ruin, a
collapsed building, smoke, fire, a road of Roman construction, a
milestone.

DISTINGUISHING FEATURES (all must be present): long stone-walled
terraces on two slopes; a row of low vines; a small UNWALLED village
of flat-roofed houses on the far ridge, small in the frame; not one
human figure anywhere. This is the only plate of this group that is
pure inhabited landscape.

PLATE 33.

THE CLAIM OF THIS PLATE: this is a Roman civic ceremony of city
foundation, performed calmly. It is not a battle, not a triumph and
not an execution. The whole content is one man walking behind a
plough drawn by a yoked ox and cow, cutting a shallow furrow across
open ground.

SUBJECT: a foundation ceremony on cleared, level ground, seen at eye

draped wool, plain oval shields resting on the ground, weathered
fallen building stone.

SETTING: open cleared ground on a hilltop, ruins low on the horizon,
bare parchment sky.

ADDITIONAL NEGATIVE FOR THIS PLATE: any figure facing the viewer, any
face visible, a portrait, a close-up of a person, a figure larger or
more detailed than the others, a crowd, prisoners, chains, violence,
drawn weapons, fire, smoke, a temple, a statue of a god, an altar, a
standard or eagle, a laurel wreath, readable letters of any kind.

DISTINGUISHING FEATURES (all must be present): a wooden plough drawn

PLATE 34.

THE CLAIM OF THIS PLATE: two things are true at once and both must be
visible — a cave mouth high in a desert cliff, and a Roman camp on
the clifftop above it. The plate shows WHERE they are, and nothing
about what anyone intended. Nobody is watching, nobody is waiting,
nobody is guarding.

SUBJECT: a desert canyon seen at eye level from a rock ledge on the
opposite side. Across the gorge, a sheer bedded limestone cliff fills
most of the frame; high in its face, well below the rim and far above
the dry gorge floor, the dark mouth of a large cave opens, its
interior going to deep near-black. On the flat plateau at the top of
that same cliff, directly above the cave, the low straight earth
rampart and squared corner of a military camp enclosure, seen edge-on
and small. The gorge floor below is bare and dry.

PERIOD MATERIAL: bedded desert limestone, a low earth-and-stone camp
rampart, dry stream-bed boulders.

SETTING: Judaean desert canyon. Bare parchment sky. Sparse dry scrub
as the only accent green.

ADDITIONAL NEGATIVE FOR THIS PLATE: any human figure, a sentry, a
soldier, a lookout, tents drawn in detail, ropes, ladders, a path or
stair or ledge route down the cliff, skeletons, bones, bundles or
scrolls, smoke, fire, birds, arrows, leader lines, any white or drawn
guide line.

DISTINGUISHING FEATURES (all must be present): one large dark cave
mouth in a sheer cliff face; a camp rampart on the rim directly above

PLATE 35.

THE CLAIM OF THIS PLATE: the coins were struck over Roman coins, and
that must be visible. On the coins in the pile, faint traces of an
earlier design — the edge of a laureate head, a curve of an earlier
rim legend — show through and are clearly out of alignment with the
new design over them. A plain new coin would make this plate say
nothing.

SUBJECT: a plain wooden table in a village room, seen at eye level
from slightly above the table edge. On it: two papyrus documents
lying open and slightly overlapping, their surfaces covered in fine
illegible ink strokes; a reed pen and a small inkwell; a pair of
small hand-held balance scales resting on the boards; and a low
loose pile of about fifteen silver coins, several lying face up. On
the face-up coins a simple design of a bunch of grapes, and across
and beneath it the faint misaligned ghost of an earlier portrait and
rim. A closed wooden document case at the table's edge.

PERIOD MATERIAL: rough plank table, papyrus, reed pen, clay inkwell,
bronze hand balance, struck silver coins with uneven flans, a plain
wooden case.

SETTING: interior of a plain village room, plastered rubble wall
behind, one doorway open to flat outdoor daylight. No lamp, no shaft
of light.

ADDITIONAL NEGATIVE FOR THIS PLATE: readable letters, numerals or
words in any script or language, letterlike ornament, gold, metallic
shine or glint, a money chest, heaps of coins, a person, hands, a
seated scribe, a soldier, weapons.

DISTINGUISHING FEATURES (all must be present): two open documents
whose writing is illegible strokes only; a reed pen and inkwell;
small hand balance scales; a pile of silver coins in which several
show BOTH a grape design AND the faint misaligned ghost of an
earlier portrait beneath it. Nobody is in this plate.
```
